Privacy

Privacy policy

SiteSnapshot is operated by Vellumray. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how to contact us about your data.

What we collect

  • account details you provide through Google sign-in, such as your email address and profile name
  • snapshot requests, including URLs, uploaded screenshots, job history, and generated files
  • billing and transaction details needed to confirm credit purchases
  • basic diagnostics and analytics used to keep the service working and measure site performance

How we use it

  • to authenticate your account and show your saved jobs and credits
  • to process snapshots, store generated files, and deliver preview or download links
  • to handle billing, support, abuse prevention, reliability, and debugging
  • to improve the product and understand which pages and flows are working

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full payment card numbers on SiteSnapshot.

Storage and retention

Account records, snapshot requests, uploaded source files, and generated artifacts may be stored so the service can deliver downloads, keep job history, and handle support requests. If you want account or snapshot data deleted, contact support and include the email tied to your account.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run SiteSnapshot, such as hosting, storage, authentication, analytics, and payment providers, or when required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support@vellumray.com.
